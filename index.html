01 — About

I'm a Senior Software Engineer with 8+ years of experience across embedded systems and IoT, currently working on safety-critical avionics software — specifically FADEC (Full Authority Digital Engine Control) and ECU platforms — as a Senior Designer at Tata Consultancy Services in Bengaluru.

My background spans the full embedded development lifecycle: system requirements, architecture and modeling, implementation, verification and validation — including structural coverage analysis across Statement, Branch, and MC/DC — and production acceptance testing. Earlier in my career I built IoT hardware and firmware from the ground up: custom PCBs, biometric sensor libraries, and cloud-connected devices. That gives me a systems-level view from silicon to software.

I care about writing scalable, verifiable code, and about hardware that's reliable and upgradeable. Outside of work, I explore emerging IoT and ML applications, listen to music, and ride.
